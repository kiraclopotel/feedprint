Taking part in a feed panel

What you will do

You open a new account on one platform in a browser profile used only for this and Feedprint records that account’s feed. You scroll the feed 30 minutes a day for 14 days without interacting, then send the file on day 15; several volunteers also use new accounts so the group can compare the files item by item.

Before day 1

  1. In Chrome, make a new profile and use it only for the panel.
  2. In that profile, add Feedprint from the Chrome Web Store. A setup tab opens by itself.
  3. Press "Begin setup", choose "Relaxed" and press "Start". Do not choose "Strict" for the panel: Strict sends nothing, so its record gets no outside time stamps. Add no licence key: the panel needs none.
  4. In the same profile, make a new account on the platform the group names, with the details the group gives you, and follow nobody.
  5. Open Feedprint, press "Open your record", go to "Export", type the code the group gives you in "Your name on the file", then press "Save". A code, not your name: the name goes on the first page of the file and into its file name.

Every day, days 1 to 14

  1. Open the panel profile and the platform's main feed: "For You" on TikTok and X, the home feed on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
  2. Check that the Feedprint window shows the platform name followed by "recording".
  3. Scroll for 30 minutes at the time of day the group set, keeping that tab in front. Posts shown while the tab is in the background can be missed.
  4. Do not like, comment, share, follow, search, send a message or open a profile. Messages are never recorded.
  5. Do not use this profile for anything else.

If something goes wrong

  • If the Feedprint window does not say "recording", reload the page.
  • If you miss a day, write down which day and carry on, and tell the group on day 15.
  • Do not remove Feedprint and do not delete the profile before day 15. Deleting them removes the record and the key that signs it.
  • The folder "Downloads/FeelingWise" holds a sealed daily backup, not the file to send.

Day 15

  1. You open Feedprint, press "Open your record", and go to "Export".
  2. You choose "The whole record", which includes every record and every month in one file. You do not choose "To show someone: the report", which holds only flagged items.
  3. The file saves to your downloads as an HTML file whose name begins "feedprint-whole-record-". It carries its own checker and opens in a browser on a computer.
  4. You send the file to the group using the method the group told you and paste the line shown after the export, which includes the file’s name, size and SHA-256.
  5. You keep your own copy until the group confirms the file arrived and its check passed.

What the file holds about you

The file holds the full text of what the feed showed, the names and addresses of the accounts that posted it, and the groups you were in. Its first page names your operating system, browser, language and time zone, and the code you typed; never your computer's name or your internet address. The file says of itself that it carries no address and no account of yours but identifies you anyway, from what was shown to you.

What leaves your computer

At "Relaxed" with no licence key, nothing leaves the computer during the 14 days except a fingerprint of the record, with no post text, sent to two of the three time-stamp services, which see your internet address and the time. You may stop at any time. Feedprint sends the file nowhere by itself, and it goes to the group only if you send it.
