What FeelingWise does NOT measure

FeelingWise records the posts it processed, and how its built-in filter, or the AI where a license key turned the AI reading on, classified retained items. It records posts and comments on the feed pages it works on, whether they are public or visible only to the account's followers; it never runs on private messages or chats. FeelingWise does NOT measure the user's emotional state. FeelingWise does NOT observe real-world relationships, family dynamics, or prior history. FeelingWise interpretations are uncertainty-aware signals; they may be challenged on their own merits.

Time stamps from outside services (RFC 3161)

An anchor is a timestamp signed by an authority that has no stake in this record. It signs a hash of the chain at one position, so everything written up to that position cannot have been written afterwards. This page checks each timestamp in your browser, offline: that it was stamped over the hash shown, that the signature holds under the certificate inside the token, and that the certificate was issued for time stamping. None of these authorities is a qualified trust service provider under EU Regulation 910/2014, so no legal presumption about the time attaches to them.
