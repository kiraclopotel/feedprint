# Subsystem: read-this

> Traced from code, 2026-09-05. Confirmed defects live in the defect register `known-issues.md`, which is not part of the public copy; [KNOWN-LIMITS.md](../KNOWN-LIMITS.md) carries what a reader outside the project needs from it. Design: `docs/design/ADULT-MODE-2026-09-05.md`.

> 9 Oct 2026: the popup no longer arms this. "Read this for me" came off Home on the owner's word; the code below stays and nothing starts it. Bringing it back is a popup button writing `READ_ARMED_KEY` (src/storage/read-arm.ts) with the time.

## Purpose

Adult mode's reading aid in the recorder: it answers when asked and is silent otherwise. The popup's "Read this for me" card arms it; the visible tab shows the crosshair Off's picker uses; a click on a post or a comment sends that one item (its text, its kind, its platform and, for a comment, the post it replies to) through the measured adult judge, unchanged, and renders the five fields as four flat lines beside the item, with one line from the record under them when the chain holds the account. The item itself is not marked, rewritten or hidden; the answer card carries the machine-readable AI mark. The reading is written to the chain as a signed pass record whose reason names it (`pass:read-this:...`), so nothing the product did not do is asserted and no rate pools it with an automatic verdict. Off does not carry it (the file imports the AI client, which `scripts/check-off-bundle.mjs` forbids in `dist-off/content.js`).

## Key files

- src/content/markers/read-this.ts - `startReadThis(platform, settings)` (the storage listener; no-op in Off and in an inspection tab; the arm is ignored unless the fresh `settings.mode` is `adult`; the key's removal, which any window's completed pick causes, disarms every other window that armed on the same press), `itemFor(boundary, platform)` (the item's own text from `TEXT_SCOPE_SELECTORS` minus bylines and nothing else: an item with no text scope, a picture or a video with no caption, is refused rather than read from the box's visible text; kind by `isComment`: Facebook through the adapter's own `isFacebookCommentLabel` stem rule, which covers every Facebook language and replies, TikTok when the boundary box holds a `comment-level-N` paragraph, Instagram and YouTube by selector; the parent post's text by `PARENT_SELECTORS`, read inside the enclosing post container on Facebook and Instagram and from the page's one open video on TikTok and YouTube; a post's `postUrl` from `LINK_SELECTORS` or the page, a comment's `postUrl` the page and its `anchorUrl` the link; the author from the boundary), `answerLines(verdict, strings)` (the four lines, or one), the answer card (`data-fw-read-answer`, mounted after the item's text scope or at the end of the item, its own style tag, a close button), the record line (`GET_AUTHOR_WEEK`), `readPassReason(verdict)` and the chain write: always `PASS_LOG`, `passReason` `read-this:charged:<effect>:<intent>` or `read-this:not-charged`, the family as the technique with the severity, `aiSource: 'cloud'`, `aiModel` and `aiProvider` as the server named them on the reply to the reading's own call (`callProvenanceFor` on an abort signal of the reading's own, because the proxy can answer another model than the one asked and names its upstream), not a constant and not the client's last-call global, `kind` post or comment. ## Inputs

`fwReadArmedAt` (a number) in `chrome.storage.local`, written by the popup; the click's boundary from `boundaryFor`; `getSettings()` (mode, `aiAnalysisEnabled`, `dailyCap`, `features`, `uiLanguage`) and `getLicense()` (tier) at pick time; the judge's reply; the service worker's week count.

## Outputs

One `callAI` per ask (`JUDGE_V1_MODEL`, `JUDGE_V1_SCHEMA`, `JUDGE_V1_ADULT_SYSTEM`, the user message from `buildJudgeV1UserPrompt`); the answer card in the page; one chain record per answered ask; a toast for each refusal (`readRefused`, `readNeedsKey`, `readAiOff`, `readFailed`). No `AUTHOR_UPDATE`, no `NEUTRALIZATION_COMPLETE`, no `SCAN_LOG`: the reading feeds no verdict, no counter and no rate.

## Invariants & contracts

- The instrument is unchanged: the reading and the automatic adult path share `askJudgeV1`; the prompt hash, model, schema and message shape are the ones `tests/unit/ai/judge-v1-adult.test.ts` locks, and that file locks the read path too. The reading applies no fire cut because nothing is acted on; the five fields are shown as returned.
- Only the picked item's text leaves the device, plus its kind, its platform and, for a comment, up to 600 characters of the post it replies to; never the author, never the page around it (`itemFor`, `buildJudgeV1UserPrompt`).
- The answer names a mechanism, never the author: the family maps to `readMechanism`, and no string in the `read*` set names a person (lock: the `answerLines` case in `tests/unit/content/markers/read-this.test.ts`).
- A free install or a master breaker off sends nothing and says why in the page (`readNeedsKey`, `readAiOff`); a failed call removes the waiting card and says so; a click on a box or on an item with fewer than 12 characters of text is refused with `readRefused`.
- The answer card carries the machine-readable AI mark (`src/content/markers/ai-mark.ts`, the EU AI Act, Article 50(2)): once the model's answer is shown the card gets `data-fw-ai-generated="reading"` and `data-fw-ai-model` naming the model the reply to the reading's own call named (absent when none did). The waiting card is not marked, and the item beside it never is: its words are its author's.
- The record line is read-only over the chain (`GET_AUTHOR_WEEK`); it is shown when the account came up at least once this week and it changes nothing downstream. It reads the live store only: for up to seven days after the UTC month flip the archived month is not counted, so the line can under-count in that window. Its word is "flagged", because that is what it counts.
- The reading's record asserts nothing the product did not do: it is a pass record, because nothing on the page was acted on, and a charged reading below the automatic path's fire cut therefore never enters the chain as a flag the measured rule would not fire. Its `detectionMode` is `pass:read-this:charged:<effect>:<intent>` or `pass:read-this:not-charged` (the logger stamps `pass:<reason>`; `input.detectionMode` is not read on the pass path). Readers of the chain that compute a rate exclude readings by the substring (`isReadingRecord`); the week count does; the dashboard's pass list shows the record with its technique and does not yet label it; the research scripts under `scripts/corpus/` filter on `detectionMode`.
- Off never arms (`isOffProduct()`), an inspection tab never arms, a non-adult mode ignores the arm, an arm older than 60 s or in a hidden tab is ignored; when two visible windows arm on one press, the first completed pick removes the key and the other window stands down.

## Should vs actual

- The design's line 2 ("It works by contempt: the 'these people' and the mockery in the last line") quotes the item. The judge returns five fields and no reason text, and a reason field would be a new instrument, so line 2 names the family's mechanism in fixed words. Quoting the item waits for a measured judge that returns a reason.
- The dashboard does not yet label a reading or filter by it; the record carries the field for when it does.
- The glossary (design section 4) is not built: its licence is unread.
- The design's section 6 says the reading is recorded "like any other" record with a new `detectionMode`. Built as a pass record with the reason as the mode, for the reason given under invariants; the family, severity, effect and intent are all in the record, in the technique and the reason rather than in a flagged record's `why`.
